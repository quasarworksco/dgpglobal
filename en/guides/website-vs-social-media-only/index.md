"I already have Facebook and Instagram, why do I need a website?" It's a common and fair question. Social media is free, easy to use, and your customers are there. But it serves a different purpose than a website, and relying on it alone has risks.

## What social media does well

- **Keeps you top of mind** with your current followers.
- **Shows your day-to-day**: jobs, news and promotions.
- **Lets you talk** with customers through messages and comments.

It's excellent for people who **already know you**. The problem is the people who don't know you yet and are searching for what you do.

## What social media doesn't do

**1. It doesn't get you found on Google.** When someone searches "electrician in Tampa", Google shows websites and Google Maps profiles first. Your Facebook page rarely makes the top results.

**2. You don't own it.** The platform decides who sees your posts, can change the rules or lock your account. A website on your own domain belongs to you.

**3. It builds less trust.** For many customers, especially for bigger purchases (construction, healthcare, professional services), a business without its own website looks small or temporary.

**4. Information gets buried.** Your services, prices and service areas get lost between posts. On a website, customers find everything in seconds.

## When social media might be enough

If your business is very small, sells mostly to people you know, and you don't plan to grow beyond that circle, social media can work for a while. As soon as you want new customers who find you on Google, you'll need a website.

## The ideal: make them work together

The combination that works best:

- **A website** as your online "office": services, photos of your work, reviews, and call and WhatsApp buttons.
- **A Google Business Profile** to show up on the map.
- **Social media** to stay visible and send traffic to your website.

Put your website link in your Instagram bio and on your Facebook page, and link your social profiles from your website. Each channel feeds the others.
