Every day, thousands of people type things like "plumber near me", "house cleaning in Orlando" or "auto repair in Houston" into Google. If your business doesn't show up for those searches, those customers call your competitors. The good news: **showing up isn't a mystery**, and most of it can be done in a month if you follow the right order.

## Week 1: your Google Business Profile

It's free, and it's the fastest way to reach nearby customers because it puts you on the map.

- Create or claim your Google Business Profile and **verify it**.
- Choose the right **primary category** (the one that describes exactly what you do) and add secondary categories.
- Fill in everything: hours, phone, service areas, list of services and a natural description that mentions your city.
- Upload at least **10 real photos**: finished jobs, your team, your shop or your vehicle.

For the detailed walkthrough, read our guide [how to show up on Google Maps](/en/guides/how-to-show-up-on-google-maps/).

## Week 2: a website that converts

Google Maps puts you on the map, but customers almost always check your website before calling. Your site should:

- Load fast on mobile.
- Say on the first screen **what you do, where, and how to reach you**.
- Keep a call button and a WhatsApp button always visible.
- Include a page or section for each main service, with your city in the text.

Link your website from your Google profile, and your profile from your website. They work better together.

## Week 3: reviews, reviews, reviews

Reviews move your position on the map more than anything else, and they're what convinces a new customer.

- Ask **every happy customer** for a review the same day you finish the job, using Google's direct link.
- Set a goal: **10 new reviews** this month.
- Reply to all of them, including the negative ones, calmly and professionally.

Our guide [how to get more Google reviews](/en/guides/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/) has ready-to-use messages for asking.

## Week 4: show you're active

Google rewards businesses that stay active.

- Post on your Google profile **once a week**: a recent job, an offer or a tip.
- Add new photos every week.
- Make sure your name, phone and address are **identical** on Google, your website, Facebook, Instagram, Yelp and Bing.

## What to expect at the end of the month

In 30 days you'll have a complete profile, a website that converts, new reviews and steady activity. Many businesses start getting their first calls from Google during these weeks, and rankings keep improving in the following months if you keep up with reviews and posts.
