Many business owners tell us: "I have a website, but nobody contacts me through it." Almost always the problem isn't the website itself, but one or more of these mistakes.

## 1. It's not clear what you do

In the first 3 seconds, visitors should know **what you offer and where**. Phrases like "Comprehensive solutions of excellence" say nothing. Better: "House and office cleaning in Orlando".

## 2. No easy way to contact you

A phone number hidden in the footer loses customers. Keep **call and WhatsApp buttons** visible at all times, especially on mobile.

## 3. It looks bad on mobile

Most of your customers visit from their phone. If they have to zoom in or the buttons are tiny, they leave.

## 4. Slow loading

If your site takes more than 3 seconds to load, many visitors close it before they see it. Oversized images are the most common cause.

## 5. No security padlock

If the browser shows "Not secure" next to your address, customers get suspicious. An SSL certificate (the padlock) is a must.

## 6. Stock photos that aren't yours

Generic photos are easy to spot. **Real photos** of your work, your team and your customers build far more trust.

## 7. Zero reviews or testimonials

People trust what other customers say. Show your Google reviews and named testimonials.

## 8. Outdated information

A "© 2018" in the footer, old hours or expired promotions make the business look abandoned.

## 9. Only one language

In Florida and Texas, a huge share of your customers speak Spanish and another share speaks English. A bilingual website doubles who you can sell to.

## 10. Too much going on

Sliders, animations, pop-ups, ten different buttons… visitors get lost. A clear page with one goal per section (call, message, request a quote) converts better.
