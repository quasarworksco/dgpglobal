Hiring a contractor is a big decision: the customer is letting someone into their home or business and paying a significant amount. That's why they check your website closely before calling. Here's what they need to find.

## 1. Your services, clear and separate

Construction, kitchen remodeling, roofing, painting, HVAC… Each main service deserves **its own section or page** with a short description. Google shows you for more searches, and customers find exactly what they need.

## 2. A gallery of real work

Before-and-after photos of your projects convince more than anything. Organize them by type of job and add a line with the city ("Bathroom remodel in Cape Coral").

## 3. Licenses and insurance up front

Show your **license number** and that you're insured. In Florida and Texas customers check, and it puts them at ease. It's an advantage over contractors who don't show it.

## 4. An estimate request form

A short form (name, phone, type of job, city, optional photos) plus a call button. Ask only for what you need: every extra field reduces requests.

## 5. Service areas

List the cities and counties you cover. It helps Google show you to nearby customers and avoids calls from places you don't serve.

## 6. Reviews and testimonials

Google reviews with names and, if possible, photos of the job. A video testimonial from a happy customer is gold.

## 7. Warranty and how you work

Explain your process: visit, estimate, contract, work and warranty. Knowing what to expect reduces the fear of hiring.

## 8. Bilingual

Many of your customers, and sometimes property owners, prefer one language or the other. A website in English and Spanish opens both markets.

## 9. A call button always visible

Many customers call from their phone in the middle of an emergency (a leak, an A/C that won't cool). Make calling you a single tap.
