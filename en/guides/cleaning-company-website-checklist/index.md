When someone needs a cleaning service, they grab their phone, search "house cleaning near me" and decide within minutes who to contact. They don't pick the cheapest or the biggest company: they pick the one that **earns their trust fastest**. Your website is what has to make that happen.

This is the checklist we use when we design websites for cleaning companies. See how many points yours covers.

## 1. Your services, explained in one line each

Residential, commercial, move-in/move-out, deep cleaning, offices, vacation rentals. Customers want to confirm in seconds that you do exactly what they need. Skip long paragraphs: a heading and one clear line per service.

## 2. A way to request a quote without calling

Many people would rather not call. Offer a **short form** (cleaning type, home or office size, city and date) and a **WhatsApp or text button** with a pre-written message. Fewer steps means more quote requests.

## 3. The areas you serve

List the cities and neighborhoods you cover. It helps twice: customers instantly know whether you come to them, and Google understands where to show your business when people search in that area.

## 4. Real reviews

Before letting someone into their home or office, people read reviews. Show your Google reviews and link to read more. If you only have a few, ask your current customers for one this week.

## 5. Before and after photos

A good before-and-after photo beats any amount of copy. Use photos of real jobs, well lit, without any personal details from your clients.

## 6. Insurance, licenses and a guarantee

If your company is insured or licensed, show it. If you offer a guarantee ("if something isn't right, we come back for free"), make it big. These details separate a professional company from a side hustle.

## 7. Fast on mobile

Most of your visitors come from a phone. If the site is slow, they leave for a competitor. Light images, no filler text and big buttons.

## 8. English and Spanish, if you serve both

In Florida many cleaning companies serve both American and Latino customers. A version in each language opens both doors, and Google can show each version to the people searching in that language.

## 9. The same contact details everywhere

Use the same business name, phone number and address on your website, Google Business Profile, Facebook and Instagram. That consistency helps Google trust your business and show it more on Maps.

## 10. A complete Google Business Profile

It isn't part of your website, but it works hand in hand with it. Fill in categories, hours, service areas and photos, and post something every week. Link your profile to your website and your website to your profile.
