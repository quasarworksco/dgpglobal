**SEO** stands for search engine optimization: everything you do to show up on Google. **Local SEO** is the part focused on searches in your area, like "dentist in Kissimmee" or "auto shop near me". For a business that serves customers in its city, it's what matters most.

## How Google decides who to show

Google uses three main criteria for local results:

1. **Relevance:** does your business offer what the person is looking for? Google reads this from your category, your services and the text on your website.
2. **Distance:** how close are you to the searcher, or to the city they typed?
3. **Prominence:** how well known and trusted is your business? Reviews, mentions on other sites and the quality of your website all count.

You can't change distance. Relevance and prominence, you can.

## The 6 pieces of local SEO

**1. A complete Google Business Profile.** The right category, services, hours, photos and posts. It's the foundation. Read [how to show up on Google Maps](/en/guides/how-to-show-up-on-google-maps/).

**2. A website that mentions your city.** Your site should say what you do and where, naturally: "Plumbing in Orlando and Kissimmee". A section for each service helps a lot.

**3. Steady reviews.** Quantity, frequency and quality. Reply to every one. Read [how to get more Google reviews](/en/guides/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/).

**4. Identical details everywhere.** The same name, phone and address on Google, your website, Facebook, Yelp, Bing and directories. This is called "NAP" (name, address, phone).

**5. Directories and mentions.** Being listed in trusted directories (chamber of commerce, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps) confirms to Google that you exist.

**6. Speed and mobile.** A slow website, or one that looks bad on a phone, loses rankings and customers.

## How long it takes

Changes to your Google profile show up within weeks. Everything else (reviews, mentions, content) builds up month after month. It isn't magic: it's consistency. For a first-month plan, read [how to get customers from Google in 30 days](/en/guides/how-to-get-customers-from-google-in-30-days/).

## Common mistakes

- Using several different addresses or phone numbers.
- Wrong categories on Google.
- A website that doesn't mention your city or your services.
- Buying reviews: Google detects them and penalizes you.
