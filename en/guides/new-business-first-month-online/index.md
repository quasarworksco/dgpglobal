You just registered your company and want to start selling. There's a lot to do, and it's easy to waste time on things that don't matter yet. This is the order that works best for most businesses.

## Week 1: your brand foundation

- **Name and logo.** A simple, professional logo that looks good small (Instagram profile) and big (sign, truck). Read [how much a professional logo costs](/en/guides/how-much-does-a-professional-logo-cost/).
- **Colors and fonts.** Pick 2 or 3 colors and use them everywhere. A simple brand guide keeps every design consistent.
- **Domain.** Buy your web address before someone else does. Read [how to choose a domain name](/en/guides/how-to-choose-a-domain-name/).

## Week 2: your website and email

- **A website** with your services, photos, service area, and call and WhatsApp buttons. You don't need something huge: a clear page that converts.
- **Professional email** on your domain (info@yourbusiness.com). It builds trust with every quote.

## Week 3: Google and social media

- **Google Business Profile**, to show up on the map. It's free. Read [how to show up on Google Maps](/en/guides/how-to-show-up-on-google-maps/).
- **Instagram and Facebook** with your logo, the same description and a link to your website.
- **The same details everywhere**: identical name, phone and address.

## Week 4: your first customers and reviews

- **Tell your network**: family, friends, past customers and contacts. Ask them to share.
- **Business cards and flyers** with a QR code to your website.
- **First reviews**: ask every customer to leave their opinion on Google. Read [how to get more Google reviews](/en/guides/how-to-get-more-google-reviews/).

## What to avoid

Many new businesses rush into paid ads before they have the basics. If your website doesn't convince or you have no reviews, the ad money goes to waste. Foundation first, advertising second.
